# Prime Canon

The shared universe begins from one protected principle: civilization is vast,
documented imperfectly, and interpreted through institutions with competing
motives.

All contributed lore is non-core unless promoted by maintainers. Non-core
documents may introduce locations, agencies, species, technologies, incidents,
and mysteries, but they must leave room for later contributors to reinterpret
or refine details.

Core canon documents cannot be edited through the public submission interface.
